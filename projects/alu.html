2-Bit ALU & Control Unit

Combinational logic from an equation

An arithmetic logic unit that computes F = 4.5A² + ∛(9B) for 2-bit inputs and shows the result on 7-segment displays. Simulated in Multisim and built on a breadboard.

Role
Group leader. Built my own ALU and compiled the group design
Year
2024
Team
Group of five
Context
Digital Electronics, APU.

Overview

I turned the equation into a 16-row truth table, minimised each output bit with Karnaugh maps, and implemented the result with logic gates driving three 7-segment displays.

The group designed a decoder-based control unit so only one member's ALU is active at a time. I assembled the final combined circuit and ran its simulation.

What I did

  1. 01Built the truth table, K-maps and output expressions.
  2. 02Built the Multisim simulation: all 16 input combinations correct.
  3. 03Built the physical breadboard version: 8 of 16 values displayed correctly, which I traced to wiring and leakage issues and documented.
  4. 04Compiled the group control unit and integration.

Signal path

  1. A, B (2-bit)
  2. Truth table
  3. K-maps
  4. Logic gates
  5. 7-segment output
