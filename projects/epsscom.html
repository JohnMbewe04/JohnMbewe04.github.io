Protection coordination for a 132/11 kV substation

A protection study for a 132/11 kV industrial (data-centre) substation with distributed energy resources, modelled in ETAP.

Outcome
Representing APU · finals 24 Oct 2026, UNITEN Putrajaya
Role
Owner of Question 3: the protective scheme and coordination study
Year
2026
Team
Team of five: Lek Yong Bin, Lau Yi Jing, Chisomo Mbewe, Wong Che Kai, Farhan Aziz Umrani
Context
National power system simulation competition organised by IEEE PES Malaysia.

Overview

The team modelled a 132/11 kV substation feeding ring main units and low-voltage feeder pillars, with distributed generation connected at 11 kV. Each member owned one competition question.

My part is the protection scheme: which relay protects each zone, the current transformer and relay schedule, the overcurrent and earth-fault settings, and showing on time-current curves that the devices trip in the right order. I also covered how the distributed generation changes fault currents and why that calls for directional elements.

What I did

  1. 01Defined the protection philosophy by zone. On the 132 kV incomers: line differential (87L) as main protection, with distance (21) and directional earth fault (67N) as backup.
  2. 02Built the protection single-line diagram in ETAP with relays on every breaker.
  3. 03Set phase overcurrent (51) and earth-fault elements and plotted the time-current curves for coordination.
  4. 04Assessed the effect of distributed generation on protection, including directional protection needs.
