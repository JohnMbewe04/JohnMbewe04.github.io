W.A.V.E

Water Assessment Vessel for Ecosystems

A solar-powered boat that measures water quality across a whole fish pond or canal and uses on-board AI to tell farmers what the readings mean.

Outcome
Grand Champion · AgriXcelerate 2026
Role
Firmware, sensor calibration and the live web dashboard

Group Design Project, APU. Entered in AgriXcelerate 2026 (Sustainability category).

Overview

Smallholder fish farmers check their water by eye or with occasional spot samples, and lab results take days. Runoff after rain or an overnight drop in water quality goes unnoticed until the fish die.

W.A.V.E is an 80 × 48 × 24 cm 3D-printed PETG catamaran with twin jet drives, a 50 W solar panel with MPPT charging and a sealed IP65 electronics bay. An eight-in-one RS-485 sonde under the hull reads pH, conductivity, ORP and temperature as the boat moves.

Engine 1, a 100-tree Random Forest running on the ESP32-S3, sorts the water into five classes (A pristine to E toxic) aligned to Malaysia's National Water Quality Standards. Because it runs on the boat, it keeps working when the mobile signal drops. Engine 2, a FastAPI service, uses the boat's GPS fix to pull real fish records from GBIF and reports which local species can survive in that water, plus likely pollution sources within 5 km from OpenStreetMap.

What I did

  1. 01Wrote the dual-core ESP32-S3 firmware on FreeRTOS: Modbus RS-485 reads from the sonde, GPS, and the on-chip classifier.
  2. 02Calibrated the pH, conductivity, ORP and temperature channels against reference solutions.
  3. 03Built the 4G uplink (SIM7600) to Firebase every 20 seconds, with store-and-forward buffering so no readings are lost during signal dropouts.
  4. 04Built the Next.js dashboard: mission overview, satellite survey map, live telemetry, power management and the AI intelligence view.
