RecipeRun

AI cooking assistant

Turns cooking videos, photos and links into structured recipes, grocery lists and a step-by-step cook mode.

Overview

People save recipes from social media and then can't use them. RecipeRun takes a link, a photo or a video and turns it into a structured recipe card that can feed a grocery list and a guided cook mode.

The app uses Flutter with Provider state, Firebase Auth and Firestore, and TypeScript Cloud Functions. RevenueCat manages subscriptions with a single “premium” entitlement that unlocks cook mode, personalisation and smart ingredient swaps.

What I did

  1. 01Built the cross-platform Flutter app and the Firestore data model (profiles, preferences, recipes).
  2. 02Wrote the AI parsing pipeline that turns unstructured posts into normalised JSON (title, ingredients, steps).
  3. 03Built a routing layer that sends each job to Gemini 2.0 Flash or GPT-4o-mini depending on whether it needs speed, low cost or reasoning.
  4. 04Integrated RevenueCat purchases and entitlements, and tested them with Play Store internal testing.
  5. 05Added merged grocery lists, nearby-store maps and location and currency caching.
