Humanoid Robot CAD

SOLIDWORKS parts, assemblies and drawings

A fully articulated humanoid robot modelled in SOLIDWORKS, with exploded views and first-angle engineering drawings for every part.

Role
Group leader. My components: the head and the hand
Year
2023
Team
Group 11
Context
Engineering Design, APU.

Overview

I led the group and designed the head (head and neck) and the hand: nine parts including four three-segment fingers, a two-segment thumb and three nuts. I chose aluminium and stainless steel for the parts and justified each choice.

What I did

  1. 013D part modelling with revolves, cut-extrudes, fillets and mirrors.
  2. 02Built the hand and head sub-assemblies, then the full body assembly.
  3. 03Produced first-angle drawings with dimensions and title blocks.
