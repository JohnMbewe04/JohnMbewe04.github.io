Smart Energy Monitor

MATLAB App Designer

An app that tracks household devices, charts power use and cost, and gives energy-saving recommendations.

Role
Individual
Year

Team
Individual

Overview

The app simulates a smart meter. Users register devices with their power ratings and daily use (stored in Excel), then explore consumption and cost as tables and charts.

An analysis tab names the most power-hungry devices, writes recommendations, and predicts future use with linear regression.

What I did

  1. 01Built device registration with add, edit, search and delete, persisted to Excel.
  2. 02Added daily, monthly and yearly power-use charts (bar, pie, area).
  3. 03Added cost scatter and bubble charts with a custom data cursor.
  4. 04Built the recommendations engine and the linear-regression forecast.
