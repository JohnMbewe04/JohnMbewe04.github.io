Fuzzy-Logic Demand-Side Management

Shaping microgrid load with a fuzzy controller

A Mamdani fuzzy controller that adjusts household power use by time of day, resident type and current demand, simulated in Simulink.

Role
Individual
Year

Generation, Transmission & Distribution of Electrical Power, APU.

Overview

Unpredictable demand is a key problem for microgrids. I built a demand-side management model that aims to keep consumption below 90% of the average.

The fuzzy system has three inputs (resident type, time of day, power consumption) and one output (adjusted consumption), joined by 36 rules, then runs in a Simulink loop.

What I did

  1. 01Designed membership functions for each input and the output in Fuzzy Logic Designer.
  2. 02Wrote 36 if-then rules covering occupancy and tariff periods.
  3. 03Built the Simulink FLC model and analysed the scopes and control surfaces.
  4. 04Explained the deviations: night-time rules allowed higher use when tariffs are low. I suggested rule and membership-function tuning as the fix.
