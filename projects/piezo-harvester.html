Piezoelectric Energy Harvester

P-SSHC interface circuit in Simulink

A simulated inductorless P-SSHC harvesting interface that beats a standard full-bridge rectifier.

Overview

Piezoelectric harvesters could power implants and IoT sensors without batteries, but simple full-bridge rectifiers waste energy charging and discharging the transducer's own capacitance.

Parallel synchronised switch harvesting on capacitor (P-SSHC) flips the transducer voltage with flying capacitors instead of a bulky inductor. I modelled it in Simulink and compared it with the baseline.

What I did

  1. 01Built the transducer, rectifier and switched-capacitor flipping network in Simulink.
  2. 02Wrote the MATLAB scripts that extract the steady-state metrics.
  3. 03Compared P-SSHC with a full-bridge rectifier under the same excitation.
