Lighting Scheme Design

Four rooms in Relux and AutoCAD

Lighting designs for a literature club, tech club, culture club and common room, calculated by hand and verified with 3D photometric simulation.

Role
Individual
Year
2024
Team
Individual
Context
Electrical Power Utilisation, APU.

Overview

I drew the floor plan in AutoCAD, sized each room's fittings with the lumen method, then placed and simulated luminaires in Relux to check illuminance, uniformity and glare.

What I did

  1. 01Hand calculations of fittings per room, including maintenance and utilisation factors.
  2. 02Relux 3D models, light distribution curves and false-colour illuminance maps.
  3. 03Analysis: the literature club hit its 400 lx target. The tech club came out brighter than needed (768 lx), so I recommended fewer or dimmer fittings.

Signal path

  2. Lumen method
  3. Luminaire choice
  4. Relux simulation
