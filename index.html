  • 2024 · Engineering Software & Applications (LabVIEW)

VIII

Chapter VIII · Echoes

Beyond the bench

What I love when I am not at the workbench. These are the things that keep me curious.

Echo 01

Friends & family

Time with the people I love, and doing what I can to make them happy.

My favourite plans are simple ones: a long meal, a trip somewhere new, a celebration that gets everyone in one photo. Making the people around me happy, in small ways or big ones, is what I enjoy most.

Echo 02

New places & great structures

I travel to look at how things are built.

Skylines, bridges, heritage streets and stadiums: I look at the structure as much as the view. I keep wondering how each one was engineered, like the steel supertrees in Singapore.

Echo 03

Food: finding it, eating it, cooking it

Trying new cuisines, then trying to make them at home.

Malaysia makes this easy. I'll try anything once, from nasi lemak to sushi to a good burger, and the best ones end up as an experiment in my own kitchen. It's also part of why I built RecipeRun.

Echo 04

Games, anime, art & animation

I love the craft, the art and the feeling that animation can carry.

I play video games and watch anime, and what keeps me coming back is the artistry. A single animated scene can hold hundreds of hand-made decisions about colour, timing, light and motion, and the best ones make you feel something words can't. I admire the people who pour that much care into a frame, and the way a world, a character or a piece of music can stay with you long after it ends. That love of craft is a big part of why this portfolio looks the way it does.
